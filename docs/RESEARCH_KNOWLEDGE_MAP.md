# NeuroScan Research Knowledge Map

## Purpose

NeuroScan has run over 135 phase documents (E1–E107+, plus pre-numbered phases A/B/C/D
and post-E107 additions) hunting for a novel algorithmic improvement to 3D
brain-tumor/MS-lesion segmentation (UNet3D_v5+ family, BraTS 2023 GLI), targeting
≥1.0 percentage point Dice gain over a canonical baseline, validated on ≥3 seeds.
Nearly every mechanism attempted was killed, closed, or landed below the bar.

The purpose of this document is **not** to re-litigate that as "100+ failures." Framed
correctly, this is an unusually rich empirical map of one specific model's failure
structure, built almost entirely through causal intervention (ablation, translation,
donor-substitution, magnitude/direction decomposition) rather than correlation alone —
and the project has an exceptional, repeatedly-demonstrated habit of catching its own
mistakes (auto-classifier bugs, sign-convention errors, shared-tensor confounds,
coordinate-frame artifacts, calibration bugs, statistical confounds) before they
calcified into false conclusions.

The actionable question this document answers is: **which observations are BOTH (a)
reproducible/causally-verified AND (b) still genuinely unexplained at the mechanism
level?** Those rows — not new untested hypotheses — are the highest-value targets for
further "phenomenon hunting," because they represent real, load-bearing facts about
this model that nobody has yet explained, rather than ideas nobody has yet tried.

Statistics and numbers below are quoted from the source documents as closely as
possible; where a document does not give a specific number, this table says so rather
than inventing one.

This version was built from a full, direct read of all individual phase documents in
`docs/phases/` (via six digest passes covering E1–E13, E14–E24, the E25 SC-TAM
sub-arc + E26–E43, E44–E69, an E50 gap-fill, and E70–E84 + the project's own synthesis
docs), not primarily from the 3–4 large synthesis documents the prior version relied
on for the E1–E43 arc. The E1–E43 section below is therefore much more granular than
before; the E44–E84+ section has been cross-checked and corrected/expanded against the
same underlying phase files.

## Part 2 — E25: the SC-TAM (Candidate 6) correction chain in full sequence

This is the project's longest and most consequential self-correction chain — 16
sequential documents under the single "E25" umbrella. It is presented here as an
explicitly ordered sequence rather than folded into the main table, because the
FINAL corrected reading is materially different from — in places the literal
opposite of — what several intermediate documents concluded, and a reader who saw
only titles/verdicts would come away with the wrong picture at multiple points.

**Design.** `PHASE_E25_CANDIDATE6_SC_TAM_DESIGN.md` locks Candidate 6 (Signed
Class-Conditional Task-Aligned Margin, "SC-TAM"): signed loss `d^SC_ij =
(z_i-z_j)^T w_hat` for foreground/background pairs, derived from E24's Gate-6
finding that background voxels moved incorrectly toward the tumor side (39.1%
correct, p=0.001, significantly below chance) while tumor voxels moved correctly
(64.5%, p=0.007).

**Step 1 — C6-2 initial results (`PHASE_E25_C62_RESULTS.md`).** H1: rho=-0.4924
(correct sign, p=4.06e-13) — the first EGGO-M candidate with a correctly-signed
loss-to-Dice correlation. H2 reported "24/24 checkpoint-batches show correct mean
sign" for realized displacement. H4 (outcome): best Dice 0.9030, below both baseline
A (0.9063) and random-control E (0.9058) — missing the bar by 1.53pp. The document
itself flagged an internal tension between H2 (favorable) and Q3/Q4's near/below-chance
single-step gradient sign correctness, explicitly declining to adjudicate.

**Step 2 — Mechanism audit (`PHASE_E25_C62_MECHANISM_AUDIT.md`).** Splitting H2's

descent (correct: `tumor→+ŵ, background→−ŵ`). Found via the theoretical sanity check

project's OWN already-passing unit test had the correct convention all along — the
bug was in the *diagnostic scripts*, not in `compute_margin_loss` or the training

## Anomalies: Proven AND Unexplained (candidates for next investigation)

These are rows where the finding is causally verified/reproduced (✅, including "✅ with caveats") but the document itself does not supply a verified causal mechanism for WHY the phenomenon exists — i.e., genuine unexplained anomalies, not merely untested ideas.

1. **E48 — small lesions depend MORE on the bottleneck (ρ=−0.454, p<0.001).** Proven repeatedly: original result, re-verified fresh in E85/E86 (ρ=−0.383, p=1.02e-05, same sign/order of magnitude), and indirectly reconfirmed by four independent later methods (E90-E92 probing, E102 evidence-magnitude, E104 calibrated uncertainty, E105 Hausdorff argmax confidence) that all converge on "small-lesion information is genuinely scarce." Yet no document in the entire E48→E97 chain supplies a verified causal account of WHY the network's training dynamics produce this specific size-dependence — six independent intervention attempts to exploit it all failed, and the WHY question was explicitly never resolved, only progressively localized (to the bottleneck, then to MaxPool3d specifically). This qualifies because the phenomenon itself is airtight and the causal chain to explain it was pursued more rigorously than almost anything else in the project, yet still terminated without an actual mechanism, per E97's own final status table.

2. **E92 — MaxPool3d(2,2) specifically (not the subsequent convolution) disproportionately destroys small-lesion decodability (Δpool small=+0.127 vs large=+0.062, p=0.022).** This is a clean, causally-localized, statistically significant finding — the clearest "where" in the whole information-loss chain. But E93's three follow-up mechanism tests (winner-identity bias, rank-recovery, activation-margin) all came back null or negligible, meaning nobody has identified WHY the max operation specifically discards disproportionately more small-lesion signal than large-lesion signal at the sub-voxel level. The information appears to vanish outright rather than being present-but-misweighted, which is itself unexplained.

3. **E65/E78/E79 — the decoder reads enc1's direction component as an absolute-coordinate-bound positional key (translation Δ=0.214–0.317, ~8–9.6x larger than local permutation/smoothing).** Reproduced across at least five independent phases (E65, E74, E78, E79, E82) with consistent, large, highly significant effect sizes, and specifically shown NOT to be fixable by local smoothing (E79: only 11% damage recovery) or explained by high-frequency content (E74: relationship reverses at the bottleneck). Yet E84's own explicit contribution audit concludes no document identifies a specific mathematical/architectural reason the network learned this exact absolute-positional-binding behavior rather than a more translation-tolerant representation — only that it exists, is large, and resists the "obvious" fixes.

4. **E71 (prediction 1) — the bottleneck representation carries real, causally-grounded self-knowledge of its own ablation sensitivity (held-out ρ=0.87–0.90, partial ρ still ~0.86 after controlling for size).** This is one of the most cleanly reproduced findings in the entire project (held-out validation, two independent training runs at different data scales both confirming it, partial correlation stronger than raw). No document offers a mechanistic account of HOW or WHY this self-predictive information comes to be encoded in the bottleneck as an emergent property of ordinary segmentation training — it is measured and exploited (or attempted to be exploited) but never explained at the representational level.

5. **E89 — functional utilization U_b tracks causal necessity N_b almost perfectly (ρ up to +0.95) while gradient-norm allocation G_b does not (ρ≈0 at the same checkpoints).** This is a robust, multi-checkpoint (7 checkpoints, ρ>0.7 from epoch 5 onward), highly significant dissociation between two ways of measuring "how much the model relies on a pathway." It correctly diagnoses that E85/E88's earlier "mismatch" was a proxy artifact, but it does not explain WHY gradient-norm allocation fails so completely to track a quantity that a same-architecture same-training functional measure tracks almost perfectly — this remains an open, verified, structurally important disconnect between optimization-visible signals and actual functional reliance.

6. **E82 — local donor-substitution damage is anisotropic and distance-graded (radius 1→3: 0.0274→0.0450, ratio 0.61) rather than either fully local-equivalent or a sharp address-lookup.** E83 later explained roughly half of the cross-AXIS component of this via physical voxel-spacing anisotropy, but explicitly could not confirm or rule out a genuine residual anisotropy, and — separately from the axis question — the WITHIN-axis distance-grading itself (E82's radius 1/2/3 result along a single tensor dimension, which E83 states is unaffected by the cross-axis confound) has no offered explanation for its specific functional shape (why does damage grow specifically this way with distance, rather than a different decay law).

7. **E32 — the critical-resolution measurement α_c is a genuine, size-independent predictor of segmentation quality (full-population ρ=0.676 beating ALL 500 permutation trials; small-lesion partial Spearman ρ=+0.132, p=0.0014, surviving both permutation (0/500 exceed it) and subject-clustered bootstrap CI [0.144,0.346] excluding zero) — considered here as a candidate, not fully promoted.** This is a rigorously validated MEASUREMENT (the most rigorously validated positive finding in the entire E1-E43 arc per the project's own master report), distinguishing it from E30/E31's DTC quantities, which failed the identical randomization-control discipline. It is included here as a borderline candidate rather than a fully qualifying anomaly because the "why" gap is different in kind from the others above: α_c's predictive value beyond size is established, but no phase document ever attempted a mechanistic account of why critical resolution captures information size doesn't — it was validated as a signal and then the two attempts to build an algorithm on it (E33's design, E34's implementation) both failed for a clearly diagnosed, unrelated reason (loss-term calibration by value instead of gradient magnitude, causing catastrophic training collapse). Because the failure mode of the one algorithm attempt is fully explained (a calibration bug, not a defect in α_c itself), and no seventh intervention has since retried α_c with correct calibration, this sits at the boundary between "unexplained proven anomaly" and "validated-but-unexploited measurement" — flagged here for future consideration rather than asserted as equivalent in status to items 1-6.

## Discrepancies Found

1. **E62/E63 GO and KILL verdicts were both invalidated by a shared-tensor bug, caught by the user rather than either authoring session.** `forward_from_enc1()` fed a single, permuted `enc1` tensor into BOTH pool1's input and the decoder skip connection. Since `MaxPool3d(2, stride=2)` is provably invariant to within-cell derangement, the claimed "pooling position matters" finding (E62's 0.0226 Dice drop, p=2e-28) was mathematically impossible to attribute to pooling — the effect could only have come through the skip path. This is exactly the kind of pre-fabricated verdict-vs-evidence mismatch this task was designed to catch: a document titled "GO" (E62) and one titled "KILLED" (E63) both had to be entirely reattributed, not merely re-scored, by E64. Neither existing bit-for-bit sanity check (identity-forward reproduction) could have caught this, since a no-op permutation is unaffected by dual-role tensor reuse.

2. **The `PHASE_E48_...REVERSED_FINDING.md` (docs/phases) and the memory file `phase_e48_bottleneck_encoding_reversed_finding.md` agree on the headline number (ρ=−0.454, p<0.001) but a LATER investigation (E85/E86, in memory only, not separately filed under docs/phases) found E48's own original stored per-subject table does not reproduce exactly on fresh recompute — same checkpoint, same code (byte-for-byte verified identical), same subjects, yet dice_intact/dice_ablated differ by up to 0.209 on individual subjects. The root cause was investigated thoroughly (checkpoint identity, code diff, dataset mtime, cross-environment scipy versions, script mtime) and NOT found. The aggregate finding re-verifies robustly (fresh ρ=−0.3834 vs original −0.4541, same sign/order of magnitude), but this is a real, disclosed, unresolved data-provenance gap between two "sources of truth" for the same phase that a casual reader combining the docs/phases file and the memory file would not notice unless they read E85/E86 specifically.

3. **E80's Arm 3 finding was published, then explicitly retracted by E81 one phase later.** E80 concluded "both encoder and decoder streams are independently, additively absolute-coordinate-bound," based on a coherent joint-shift condition being WORSE than either shift alone. E81 showed this was substantially a coordinate-frame-displacement artifact (undoing the same shift on the output recovers 93.5% of the apparent damage; the network is near-equivariant, relative equivariance error only 0.096). The retraction is clearly flagged at the top of the E80 document itself, but a table built only from titles/verdicts (as this task explicitly warns against) would report E80's Arm 3 conclusion as standing fact rather than a retracted claim.

4. **E82's own summary JSON file's `local_reading` field says "LOCAL EQUIVALENCE," which the phase's own prose explicitly calls INCORRECT.** The corrected reading (a real, distance-graded, non-trivial local penalty) was reached only by manually re-running pairwise significance tests rather than trusting the script's arbitrary 0.05 threshold classifier. Similarly, E77's own summary JSON's `outcome` field says "A" (clean rare/promising tail), which the phase's own prose explicitly overturns to "A and B simultaneously" (the tail substantially IS the lesion tissue, making naive tail suppression dangerous). E80's own summary JSON's `reading` field states the "(i) encoder representation" conclusion, which the phase's own prose calls WRONG. These are three separate instances (E77, E80, E82) where an auto-generated threshold classifier's literal output was deliberately kept unedited on disk as a "faithful record" but is flatly contradicted by the human-written analysis in the same document — a downstream reader who parses only the JSON summary files (rather than the prose) would get three wrong verdicts.

5. **E104b's auto-interpretation script produced a "DISTINCT_SIGNALS" label that the phase's own write-up calls a bug**, not a genuine finding: the script's if/elif branching checked whether raw correlation exceeded a threshold BEFORE checking regression significance, so it never reached the branch that would have reported the real finding (a significant negative partial coefficient despite a null raw correlation). This was caught only by manually reading the full regression table rather than trusting the auto-classifier's label — the same class of error flagged in Discrepancy 4 above, recurring at least four times across this project's history (E77, E80, E82, E104b) with the auto-classifier consistently being the source of the wrong verdict, not the underlying data.

6. **E107's auto-script issued a "CASE_3_KILL" verdict that the phase's own write-up calls premature**, and this premature verdict was itself investigated further (not simply overridden) via a proposed floor-effect explanation — which was then ALSO tested and explicitly REFUTED (logit-space re-measurement gave the same qualitative reversal, ruling out the floor/ceiling explanation). The FINAL verdict is also "CASE_3_KILL," identical in label to the premature one, but arrived at for a verified rather than assumed reason. A reader who only sees the final "KILLED" status without the intermediate correction would miss that the project caught its own script jumping to the right label for the wrong reason, and did the extra work to confirm the label was actually correct.

7. **E45 and E54's per-subject 3-seed means (0.8939 and 0.8944 respectively) sit on opposite sides of the corrected +1pp target (0.8942) by margins of 0.0003 and 0.0002** — the `PHASE_E45_E54_3SEED_CONFIRMATION_RESULT.md` document itself explicitly warns against reporting E54 as "confirmed" and E45 as "not confirmed" on this basis, calling it "a coin-flip-level tie against the threshold for both," since the margin is smaller than one-tenth of each mechanism's own between-seed standard deviation. Any downstream table that reduces this to a simple pass/fail per mechanism (as a naive parse of "E54 MET, E45 NOT MET" would do) would misrepresent the source document's own explicit caution.

8. **The E58 donor-substitution "wrong context is worse than none" finding was initially treated as a candidate genuine phenomenon (reproduced twice, on two checkpoints, with the second replication showing a LARGER effect)**, but Stage 3's confound check (comparing against generic mean-bottleneck ablation) reversed this interpretation to "very likely a known out-of-distribution-ablation artifact (Li & Janson, NeurIPS 2024), not a novel causal finding." A table entry based only on Stages 2/2b (without Stage 3) would present this as a validated novel phenomenon rather than a probable measurement confound — exactly the kind of mid-chain reversal this task's instructions asked to be flagged explicitly.

9. **E18's "decoder-frozen reduces representation rotation" result was published as a headline finding, then reversed the same day by a follow-up pre-E19 review.** `PHASE_E18_REPRESENTATION_ROTATION_SOURCE.md`'s original verdict was "decoder learning is the dominant source of rotation," based on freeze_decoder showing the lowest rotation of any ablation by four independent geometric measures. `PHASE_E18_FOLLOWUP_PRE_E19_REVIEW.md`, written the same day, found that freeze_decoder — despite having ~64x MORE active margin mechanism than baseline (mean margin loss 0.09972 vs 0.00155) — had the LOWEST Dice among the three real freeze ablations (0.8432 vs baseline's 0.9062) and a WEAKER, non-significant margin-Dice correlation (r=-0.354, p=0.126) than baseline's own significant one (r=-0.683, p=0.0009). The follow-up states explicitly: "we successfully stabilized a representation that simply isn't good enough... this was reported in E18's own numbers but not framed this way — it should have been the headline caveat, not a footnote." A table entry built only from E18's own title/verdict, without reading the same-day follow-up, would report the opposite of the corrected conclusion.

10. **E43's boundary-localization test initially returned the OPPOSITE of its own hypothesis, and this was caught and corrected inline within the same document** (not via a separate follow-up phase, unlike the multi-document E25/C6-2 sign-bug saga or the E18/E18-followup pair). `PHASE_E43_REPRESENTATION_CHANGE.md` first found interior representation cells changed MORE than boundary cells under D4/D2 supervision — backwards from E36's boundary-ambiguity hypothesis. Investigation found a measurement confound: for small lesions, a coarse "interior" region can be as few as 3-9 cells, so "interior" mislabels boundary-adjacent regions. After restricting to scans with ≥9 reliable interior cells, the interior-vs-boundary split became statistically indistinguishable (48%/52%, "no better than a coin flip"), killing E36's specific boundary-localization claim while preserving its separate "coarse supervision reshapes its targeted representation" finding. Notable as a textbook example of the project catching its own wrong-direction result within a single document's writing process, rather than needing an external cross-check or a dedicated correction phase.

11. **E70 (CAS)'s own results document contains a title-vs-body gap: its headline Dice number looks like a win while its own gate diagnostic falsifies the claimed mechanism, both in the same file.** `PHASE_E70_RESULTS_SEED0.md` reports MM_CAS vs MM at +0.30pp with a CI excluding zero — a number that reads as a real effect — but the SAME document's own summary table marks "CAS improves Dice" as "single seed, inside noise range, unreplicated" and "CAS works by correspondence correction" as "❌ falsified by the gate diagnostic" (the gate anti-correlates with the intended sensitivity map, ρ=-0.388, unanimous 0/125 subjects in the predicted direction, p~8.2e-114). A reader stopping at the headline Dice number would miss that the document itself disclaims both the durability of the effect and the validity of the mechanism before the file ends. The 3-seed follow-up later confirmed the effect does not replicate (mean +0.24pp, std 0.22, nearly as large as the mean) — "the third time this exact pattern has appeared," after CCABA and E45/E54.

12. **E58's Stage-2/2b "donor substitution is worse than zeroing" finding is walked back by the same document's own Stage 3**, not merely superseded by a later phase (distinct from, though related to, Discrepancy 8 above): `PHASE_E58_BOTTLENECK_CONTEXT_CHARACTERIZATION.md` explicitly narrates, within itself, first flagging the Stage-2 finding as a "candidate 'genuinely new phenomenon'" and then presenting Stage 3's mean-bottleneck-ablation control as "evidence against treating the donor-substitution finding as a genuine, novel... phenomenon" — a real-time walk-back visible entirely within one document's own text, worth distinguishing from cases (like E18, E25/C6-2, and E80/E81) where the correction required a separate companion or follow-up document.

13. **E68's design document contains one of the most extensive self-retractions in the whole project, entirely in-place.** `PHASE_E68_SC_DCU_DESIGN.md`'s original Sections 1–5 claimed a novel offset-fidelity supervision approach; a follow-up novelty search (documented within the same file) found synthetic-deformation-as-ground-truth and transformation-consistency supervision both well-established prior art, and — more fundamentally — that nothing justified assuming the task-optimal offset should equal the literal geometric inverse of an imposed shift. The document's own "Correction record" table downgrades every original novelty claim to "No" or "Open," and states outright: "Do not cite Sections 1–5's novelty claim; it is retracted." A table entry sourced only from the document's title or opening sections would carry a claim the document itself later disowns in the same file.

14. **E69's initial pooled size-correlation finding is explicitly walked back within the same document once per-seed data is checked.** `PHASE_E25...` sibling document `PHASE_E69_E54_OFFLINE_MINING_RESULT.md` reports a pooled correlation (rho=-0.118, p=0.023) suggesting the cross-seed-consistent-improvement effect tracks lesion size — but the same document immediately checks per-seed data and finds "this does not hold up under scrutiny": no individual seed shows a significant size correlation, and the consistently-improved group's median lesion size is actually LARGER (97,805) than the consistently-degraded group's (77,815) — the opposite of the E48/E65 size-specificity story that motivated the check. A separate covariate (baseline Dice, p=0.020) is also flagged in the same document as failing Bonferroni correction. Both walk-backs occur inline, not via a later phase.

15. **E30 (DTC feasibility)'s own "qualified GO" verdict is explicitly flagged by the same document as potentially misleading if read at face value.** The document states plainly: "All four gates technically pass, but Gates B/C pass on a population that is the near-opposite of DTC's motivating failure mode" (its predictive signal held only for the 25.8% of components with well-defined reference mass, overwhelmingly LARGE components, nearly opposite the small-lesion population DTC was designed to help) — and explicitly warns the GO should not be read as "DTC helps small lesions... it has not yet been tested there." A table entry recording only "GO" without this caveat would misrepresent what was actually shown; the mechanism was in fact killed one phase later (E31) once tested on the population it was meant for.

16. **E52's ASR gradient-calibrated re-attempt initially looks like it could rescue E34's approach via better calibration, but the same document shows the deeper problem is direction, not magnitude — a distinction with sequencing significance for E53.** `PHASE_E52_ASR_GRADIENT_CALIBRATED_RESULT.md` fixed a real 43x gradient-magnitude blowup, and still reproduced E34's exact catastrophic collapse pattern (val Dice 0.10 at epoch 1) — meaning a table entry noting only "gradient recalibrated" without the outcome would wrongly suggest the fix worked. The document's own diagnosis (the loss term's DIRECTION, not magnitude, fights early training) is the reason E53 could be meaningfully designed around a curriculum-delay rather than further calibration tuning.
