# PHASE_E50_IECG_RESULT.md

**Summary:** IECG (Internally-Estimated Counterfactual Gating, `UNet3D_v7`) was evaluated on 3 seeds from the start, per the post-E49 multi-seed policy. Result: mean best_val_dice 0.9065 (seeds 0.9081, 0.9030, 0.9084; std 0.0030), a mean improvement of only +0.02pp over the canonical baseline (0.9063) — statistically indistinguishable from zero — and −0.31pp vs. D4-only (0.9096) and −0.29pp vs. CCABA's own 3-seed mean (0.9094, E49). The pre-declared bar (≥1.0pp mean improvement, 95% CI not overlapping D4-only) was NOT MET. The internal sensitivity-head mechanism did work as designed (s_hat tracked s_true closely per-epoch logs) but this did not translate into task benefit.

**Verdict consistency:** The stated verdict (null/negative result, "IECG does not work... not spun as a partial win") is consistent throughout the body — no contradiction or walk-back. One nuance flagged honestly within the text itself: the paired t-test comparing IECG vs. CCABA seed-matched runs does not reach significance at n=3 (p=0.11), explicitly caveated in-line as "underpowered to confirm at this sample size, not evidence of no difference" — this is a hedge on the *magnitude/certainty* of the IECG-vs-CCABA gap, not on the core verdict that IECG itself misses its own +1.0pp bar.

**Bug found/fixed:** None mentioned in this document.
