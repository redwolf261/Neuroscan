# E34-I — Literature Novelty Gate

**Status: NOT PERFORMED.**

This project's standing policy is to run a literature-novelty search before committing real training compute to an idea, as a gate before spending that compute — not as a mandatory step in an early feasibility check that never reaches a training decision.

E34 was stopped at Section C (critical small-lesion analysis) because the proposed mechanism's central empirical claim — that a Gaussian-blurred contextual field retains meaningful support where the exact tumor outline has already vanished under resizing — failed decisively for the small-lesion population (S1–S4) the mechanism was designed to help, at every pre-declared blur width, at both coarse decoder resolutions tested. See `E35_scale_context_feasibility_report.md` for the full result.

Since no training run is warranted, the literature check that would normally precede one was not performed. No claim of novelty is made or implied by this omission.
